Automation & Digital Solutions

Stop typing, start automating — modern workflows that save hours every week and give you numbers in real time.

Home  /  Services  /  Automation & Digital

Most businesses waste hours every week on manual financial admin — typing invoices, chasing payments, forwarding receipts, copying data between systems. Every one of those tasks can be automated. We design and build automation around your accounting system so the routine work happens by itself, accurately, every time.

Invoicing & Payments

  • Automated invoice processing
  • Recurring billing setup
  • Payment reminder automation
  • Integration with payment platforms

Invoices raised automatically, sent on schedule, and chased politely but persistently until they're paid — with card and bank payment links that get you paid faster.

Expenses & Documents

  • Expense management automation
  • Cloud document storage
  • Approval workflow automation

Snap a receipt on your phone and it's captured, categorised and filed automatically. Purchase approvals route to the right person without a single email chain.

Reporting & Insight

  • Financial reporting automation
  • Dashboard creation and KPI reporting

Live dashboards showing sales, cash, debtors and the KPIs that matter to your business — updated automatically, available on any device, no spreadsheet wrangling required.

Integrations & Workflow

  • Integration with e-commerce platforms
  • CRM and accounting software integration
  • Time-saving workflow automation

Selling on Shopify, Amazon or eBay? Running a CRM? We connect your platforms to your accounts so orders, fees and payouts reconcile themselves — and your systems finally talk to each other.
