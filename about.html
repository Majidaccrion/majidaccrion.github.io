Who we are

Accounting Built Around Your Business, Not Ours

Accrion Accountants was founded on a simple belief: every business — from a one-person startup to an established company — deserves the same quality of financial advice that large firms reserve for their biggest clients.

Based at Atrium House on Manchester Road in Bury, we work with sole traders, partnerships, limited companies and individuals across Greater Manchester and the wider UK. Our practice is fully cloud-based, which means your accounts are always up to date, always accessible, and always ready to answer the question that matters: how is my business really doing?

We keep our client list deliberately manageable so that every client knows exactly who they're dealing with and gets answers fast — usually the same working day, often within the hour on WhatsApp.

How we work

Our Approach

Compliance First

HMRC and Companies House deadlines are tracked for every client. Returns are prepared early, checked thoroughly and filed on time — so penalties simply don't happen.

Technology-Led

We build your finances on QuickBooks or Xero, automate the repetitive work, and give you live dashboards instead of a shoebox of receipts and a year-end surprise.

Genuinely Personal

You'll always speak to someone who knows your business. Call, email or WhatsApp us directly — no call centres, no ticket queues, no being passed around.
